Why Choose Stamped Concrete

Beauty of Stone, Durability of Concrete

Stamped concrete delivers the aesthetic appeal of premium materials—natural stone, clay brick, aged wood—without the premium price tag. Unlike pavers that shift and settle over time, stamped concrete forms a monolithic surface that resists weed growth, insect damage, and the heaving caused by Idaho's freeze-thaw cycles. Whether you're creating a resort-style patio in Meridian, an elegant driveway in Eagle, or a charming walkway in Star, stamped concrete offers unmatched versatility and lasting beauty.

Cost-Effective Luxury

Stamped concrete costs 30-50% less than natural stone or clay pavers while delivering comparable visual impact. You get the upscale appearance of $20-30 per square foot materials at $12-18 per square foot installed. The savings are even greater when you factor in lower long-term maintenance compared to pavers or wood decking.

Low Maintenance

No weed pulling between pavers, no splinters like wood decks, no sealing required annually like natural stone. Stamped concrete needs only occasional cleaning and resealing every 2-3 years to maintain its appearance. The sealed surface resists stains from grills, spills, and Idaho's seasonal debris.

Unlimited Design Options

Choose from 30+ stamp patterns, blend multiple colors, add decorative borders, or incorporate custom logos and designs. We can match your home's existing stonework, complement your roof tiles, or create an entirely unique aesthetic. The color and pattern combinations are virtually limitless.

Popular Patterns

Stamped Concrete Patterns We Offer

Ashlar Slate

Our most popular pattern for patios, pool decks, and walkways. Ashlar slate mimics cut stone blocks in varying rectangular sizes, creating an elegant, upscale appearance. The pattern works beautifully with earth-tone color combinations—tans, browns, grays, and terracotta hues. We often use a two-color application with darker grout lines and lighter stone faces for enhanced depth and realism. Ashlar slate complements both traditional and contemporary home styles throughout Meridian, Eagle, and Boise neighborhoods.

Cobblestone

Classic European charm perfect for driveways, courtyards, and entryways. Cobblestone patterns feature rounded, irregular stone shapes that evoke old-world streets and plazas. This pattern is exceptionally durable for vehicle traffic and provides excellent traction when sealed with anti-slip additives. Popular color combinations include charcoal with buff highlights, warm grays with amber tones, or multi-color blends that mimic naturally quarried stone. Cobblestone stamped driveways are increasingly popular in Eagle's custom home communities.

Brick Patterns

Timeless appeal with multiple layout options. Running bond (standard brick pattern) offers clean, linear aesthetics ideal for walkways and driveways. Herringbone creates dynamic visual interest with interlocking V-shapes, perfect for patios and courtyards. Basketweave provides a woven appearance that adds texture and sophistication. We can replicate traditional red clay brick, weathered antique brick, or modern gray brick tones. Brick patterns pair beautifully with contrasting border designs and are a favorite for Meridian homes with traditional or colonial architecture.

Random Stone & Wood Plank

Random stone patterns mimic irregular flagstone or fieldstone with natural, organic shapes—ideal for rustic or craftsman-style homes. Wood plank stamps replicate the grain and texture of hardwood decking, perfect for pool surrounds where barefoot comfort matters and real wood would warp or splinter. We can create the look of weathered barn wood, smooth ipe, or hand-scraped hardwood. These patterns are particularly popular for pool decks and covered patios throughout Star and Kuna.

How We Work

Our Stamped Concrete Process

Every stamped concrete project follows our meticulous process, ensuring beautiful results whether we're stamping a patio in Nampa or a driveway in downtown Meridian.

  1. 01
    Design consultation. We meet to discuss your vision, review pattern samples, and select color combinations. We bring physical sample boards so you can see how patterns and colors look together. We'll advise on pattern scale relative to your space and recommend color combinations that complement your home's exterior.
  2. 02
    Site preparation and forming. We excavate to proper depth, install compacted gravel base, and set forms to establish slab edges and drainage slope. Reinforcement (wire mesh or rebar) is positioned according to project requirements. All utility locates are completed before excavation begins.
  3. 03
    Concrete placement and coloring. We pour concrete to specified thickness (4 inches for patios, 5-6 inches for driveways). Color is applied using one of two methods: integral color (pigment mixed into the concrete batch) or color hardener (broadcast onto the surface and troweled in). We often combine both for rich, dimensional color.
  4. 04
    Stamping. Once concrete reaches proper consistency, we apply release agent (which prevents stamps from sticking and adds secondary color highlights) and press textured stamp mats into the surface. We work systematically across the slab, ensuring consistent impression depth and proper pattern alignment.
  5. 05
    Curing, cleaning, and sealing. After 24-48 hours, we wash off excess release agent and apply cure-and-seal product. Once fully cured (7-14 days), we apply a high-quality acrylic sealer that enhances color, adds sheen, and protects against stains and UV damage. Anti-slip additive is included for safety on wet surfaces.

Properly installed and maintained stamped concrete lasts 25-30 years or more. The key to longevity is quality installation with adequate slab thickness (4 inches minimum for patios, 5-6 inches for driveways), proper reinforcement, and regular sealing. We recommend resealing every 2-3 years to maintain color vibrancy and protect against Idaho's freeze-thaw cycles, UV exposure, and deicing salts. With proper care, stamped concrete outlasts pavers and costs significantly less than natural stone while providing similar aesthetics.

Yes, stamped concrete overlays can transform existing concrete surfaces. We apply a 1/4 to 1/2 inch polymer-modified concrete overlay that bonds permanently to the existing slab. The surface is then stamped with your chosen pattern and colored with integral pigments or surface-applied color hardeners. This option is ideal for updating weathered patios, pool decks, or walkways without the expense and mess of demolition. Existing concrete must be structurally sound—severely cracked or heaved slabs may require repair or replacement before overlay installation.

Stamped concrete can be slippery when wet if not properly finished. We address this by broadcasting non-slip aggregate (silica sand or aluminum oxide) into the sealer, creating a textured surface that provides traction even when wet. This is especially important for pool decks, walkways, and driveway approaches. The anti-slip additive is virtually invisible but significantly improves safety. We can adjust the grit level based on your preference and the surface's intended use—more texture for pool decks, less for patios where barefoot comfort matters.
