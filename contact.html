Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get an estimate?

We typically provide free estimates within 24-48 hours of your inquiry. For urgent projects, we offer same-day estimates when possible. Call us at (208) 555-0147 to schedule your estimate.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes! All our estimates are completely free with no obligation. We'll visit your property, discuss your project, take measurements, and provide a detailed written estimate.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the entire Boise metropolitan area including Meridian, Boise, Eagle, Star, Kuna, Nampa, Caldwell, and Middleton. If you're unsure if we serve your area, just give us a call.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Meridian Concrete Solutions is fully licensed to operate in Idaho and carries comprehensive general liability insurance and workers' compensation coverage for your protection.

Do you handle permits?

Yes, we handle all necessary permits and inspections for your concrete project. This includes submitting applications, coordinating with city inspectors, and ensuring your project meets all local building codes.
