Terms of Service

Last updated: January 1, 2024. These terms govern the relationship between Meridian Concrete Solutions and our clients for all concrete services provided.

1. Services Provided

Meridian Concrete Solutions provides residential and commercial concrete services including but not limited to: driveway installation and replacement, patio construction, stamped concrete, concrete repair, sidewalks and walkways, foundations and slabs, and retaining walls. All services are performed in accordance with Idaho building codes and industry standards.

2. Estimates and Quotes

We provide free, written estimates for all projects. Estimates are valid for 30 days from the date issued. An estimate is not a guaranteed price — final pricing may change if:

  • Site conditions differ from what was visible during the estimate (hidden debris, poor soil, undocumented utilities)
  • You request changes to the scope of work after approval
  • Material costs change significantly between estimate and project start (more than 10%)
  • Access issues require additional equipment or labor

Any price changes will be communicated and approved in writing before work proceeds.

3. Payment Terms

Payment terms vary by project size:

  • Projects under $5,000: Full payment due upon completion
  • Projects $5,000–$15,000: 50% deposit required to schedule, 50% due upon completion
  • Projects over $15,000: Custom payment schedule based on project milestones

We accept cash, check, and major credit cards. A 3% processing fee applies to credit card payments. Work will not commence until the required deposit is received. Final payment is due before materials or equipment are removed from the site.

4. Scheduling and Completion

We will provide an estimated start date and completion timeline. However, concrete work is weather-dependent. Rain, extreme heat, or freezing temperatures may delay pouring or require additional curing time. We will communicate any delays as soon as possible. We are not liable for delays caused by weather, material shortages, or circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

5. Property Access and Preparation

You agree to provide clear access to the work area. This includes:

  • Removing vehicles, furniture, and obstacles from the work zone
  • Ensuring gates are unlocked and accessible
  • Identifying and marking any underground utilities, sprinkler lines, or septic tanks
  • Securing pets indoors during work hours

We are not responsible for damage to items left in the work area or for undocumented underground utilities.

6. Warranties

Workmanship Warranty: We warrant our workmanship for 2 years from the date of completion. This covers defects in installation such as improper finishing, inadequate reinforcement, or failure to follow agreed specifications.

Material Warranty: Concrete materials are warranted according to manufacturer specifications, typically 1 year for standard mixes.

What's Not Covered: Normal wear and tear, cracking due to soil settlement or tree roots, damage from vehicles exceeding weight limits, damage from de-icing salts, cracks from freeze-thaw cycles if sealer was not maintained, and any damage caused by third parties or acts of nature.

Warranty Claims: To make a warranty claim, contact us in writing within the warranty period. We will inspect the issue and determine if it's covered. Warranty service does not extend the original warranty period.
