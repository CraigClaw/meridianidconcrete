Our Process: From Estimate to Completion

1. Free Estimate

We visit your property, assess conditions, discuss options, and provide a detailed written estimate within 24 hours. No cost, no obligation.

2. Design & Material Selection

We help you choose the right finish, color, and pattern for your project. We bring samples so you can see how materials look in your actual lighting conditions.

3. Scheduling

We schedule your project around your availability and weather conditions. Most residential projects are completed within 2-5 working days.

4. Construction

Our crew arrives on time, protects surrounding landscaping and structures, and completes the work with professional craftsmanship. We clean up thoroughly each day.

5. Curing & Sealing

We monitor curing conditions and apply professional-grade sealer after the appropriate curing period. Written care instructions provided.

6. Follow-Up

We follow up after 30 days and 1 year to check on your concrete and ensure complete satisfaction.
