How Much Does a Concrete Driveway Cost in Boise?

Published 2024-01-15 | By Meridian Concrete Solutions

If you are planning a concrete driveway project in the Boise metro area, one of your first questions is probably: How much will it cost? This guide breaks down the real costs of a concrete driveway in the Treasure Valley, based on current local pricing and our 15+ years of experience.

Average Concrete Driveway Cost in Boise

The average concrete driveway in the Boise metro area costs between $4,200 and $12,600, depending on size, finish, and site conditions. Here is a detailed breakdown:

What Affects Driveway Cost in Boise?

Several factors influence the final cost of your concrete driveway:

  • Size and shape — Larger driveways cost more overall but less per square foot. Complex shapes (curves, angles) add 10-20% to the cost.
  • Finish type — Broom finish is the most affordable. Exposed aggregate adds 20-30%. Stamped patterns add 50-100%.
  • Site preparation — Removing an old driveway adds $1-$3 per square foot. Grading and base preparation add another $1-$2 per square foot.
  • Accessibility — Difficult access (narrow gates, steep slopes, long distances from the street) increases labor costs.
  • Reinforcement — Wire mesh or rebar adds $0.50-$1.50 per square foot but is essential for Idaho freeze-thaw conditions.
  • Sealing — Professional sealing adds $0.50-$1 per square foot but extends driveway life significantly.

Why Boise Driveways Cost What They Do

Idaho's specific conditions affect concrete driveway pricing:

  • Air entrainment is non-negotiable. All exterior concrete in Idaho must be air-entrained (6% minimum) to survive 100+ annual freeze-thaw cycles. This is standard on all our pours.
  • Base preparation is critical. Clay soils common in Meridian and Nampa require deeper base preparation (6-8 inches of compacted gravel) than sandy soils.
  • Short season — Concrete season in the Treasure Valley runs roughly April through October. Peak demand in May-July can affect scheduling and pricing.
