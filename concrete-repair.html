Common Concrete Damage in Idaho

🧊 Freeze-Thaw Cracking

The #1 cause of concrete damage in Boise. Water enters pores and joints, freezes, expands by 9%, and gradually widens cracks. Over 100 freeze-thaw cycles each winter turn hairline cracks into major structural problems if left unrepaired.

🧂 Salt & De-Icer Damage

Sodium chloride de-icing salts attack concrete from the surface, causing scaling and spalling. Especially severe on driveways and sidewalks near roads treated by ACHD. Once the surface flakes, underlying concrete is exposed to more damage.

⬇ Settling & Sinking

Meridian and Nampa clay soils expand when wet and shrink when dry, causing slabs to settle unevenly. This creates trip hazards and poor drainage. We use mudjacking and polyurethane foam leveling to fix settled slabs.

💥 Spalling & Surface Deterioration

Surface spalling, where the top layer flakes off, is common throughout the Treasure Valley. Caused by poor air entrainment, excess water in the original mix, or de-icing salt exposure. Early repair prevents deeper damage.

Our Concrete Repair Methods

Crack Repair

For cracks up to 1/4 inch: we route and seal with polyurethane sealant that expands and contracts with Idaho temperature swings. For structural cracks: epoxy injection restores the slab to original strength. Cost: $3-$8 per linear foot depending on method.

Concrete Resurfacing

When the surface is spalled or deteriorated but the underlying slab is sound, resurfacing applies a thin polymer-modified concrete overlay that restores the surface. Can be colored or textured to match existing concrete. Cost: $4-$8 per square foot.

Slab Leveling (Mudjacking)

Traditional mudjacking pumps a cement-based slurry under settled slabs to raise them back to original position. Effective for large slabs with stable subsoil. Cost: $3-$6 per square foot.

Polyurethane Foam Leveling

Modern alternative to mudjacking. High-density polyurethane foam is injected under settled slabs and expands to lift them. Lighter than mudjacking so it won't cause further settling. Cures in 15 minutes. Cost: $5-$10 per square foot.

Partial & Full Slab Replacement

When damage is too extensive for repair, we remove and replace the affected slab. Includes proper base preparation, reinforcement, and air-entrained concrete rated for Idaho's freeze-thaw cycle. Cost: $6-$12 per square foot.
