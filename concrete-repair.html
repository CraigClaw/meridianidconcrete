Why Repair Matters

Don't Ignore Concrete Damage — It Only Gets Worse

Small cracks and minor settling might seem cosmetic, but Idaho's climate turns minor issues into major problems. Water infiltration, freeze-thaw expansion, and soil movement accelerate deterioration. Early repair saves money and extends the life of your concrete investment.

Prevents Water Damage

Cracks allow water to penetrate beneath slabs, eroding the base and causing further settling. Sealing cracks early protects the structural integrity of your concrete and prevents costly foundation issues.

Cost-Effective Solution

Repair costs a fraction of full replacement. A typical crack injection or patching job runs $300-$800, while replacing a driveway can exceed $5,000. Smart homeowners repair early and often.

Improves Curb Appeal

Cracked, spalled, or sunken concrete detracts from your home's appearance. Professional repair restores a clean, uniform look that enhances property value and neighborhood aesthetics.

Our Services

Concrete Repair Services We Provide

Crack Injection & Sealing

We use epoxy and polyurethane injection systems to permanently seal cracks from the inside out. Hairline cracks (under 1/8 inch) receive penetrating sealers, while wider cracks are routed and filled with flexible polyurethane that moves with temperature changes. This method prevents water infiltration and stops crack propagation. Ideal for driveways, sidewalks, and foundation slabs throughout Meridian and the Treasure Valley.

Concrete Resurfacing

When surface damage is extensive but the slab is structurally sound, resurfacing offers a cost-effective alternative to replacement. We apply a thin layer (1/4 to 1/2 inch) of polymer-modified concrete overlay that bonds permanently to the existing surface. Available in smooth, broom, or stamped finishes. Perfect for weathered driveways, pool decks, and patios that need a fresh look without the expense of demolition.

Mudjacking (Slab Jacking)

Sunken concrete doesn't always need replacement. Mudjacking pumps a cementitious slurry beneath settled slabs, lifting them back to their original position. We drill small (2-inch) holes, inject the material under pressure, and patch the holes seamlessly. This process restores proper drainage, eliminates trip hazards, and costs 50-70% less than replacement. Commonly used for sidewalks, garage floors, and patio slabs across Boise, Eagle, and Kuna.

Spall Repair

Spalling—where concrete surface flakes or chips away—is common in Idaho due to freeze-thaw damage and deicing salt exposure. We remove loose material, clean the area thoroughly, apply a bonding agent, and patch with high-strength repair mortar matched to your existing concrete. For larger spalled areas, we may recommend partial-depth or full-depth replacement depending on the extent of damage.

Joint Repair & Sealing

Expansion and control joints prevent random cracking, but they deteriorate over time. We remove old, failed joint material, clean the joints thoroughly, and install new backer rod and flexible sealant that accommodates thermal movement. Proper joint maintenance prevents water infiltration and extends the life of your concrete surfaces significantly.

Stair & Step Repair

Concrete stairs and steps take heavy wear and are prone to edge damage, cracking, and settling. We repair broken nosings, level settled steps, and restore handrail anchors. Safety is paramount—uneven or damaged steps create serious trip hazards. Our repairs restore both function and appearance to entryway stairs, deck steps, and landscape stairways.
