Our Story

About Meridian Concrete Solutions

We're not just another concrete contractor — we're your neighbors. Since 2010, we've been building driveways, patios, and foundations across the Treasure Valley with the same care we'd give our own properties.

Idaho Roots, Local Expertise

Meridian Concrete Solutions was founded in 2010 by a team of concrete professionals who grew up in Idaho's Treasure Valley. We understand this region's unique challenges — from the expansive clay soils in south Meridian to the rocky terrain in Eagle's foothills, from brutal freeze-thaw cycles that crack poorly installed concrete to the intense summer sun that fades inferior sealers.

We've poured concrete in every neighborhood from Tuscany Village to Promontory, from downtown Boise's historic districts to new construction in Star. That local knowledge isn't something you can learn from a manual — it comes from years of working in these specific conditions, learning what works and what doesn't.
