Our Story: Building Idaho's Foundations Since 2010

Meridian Concrete Solutions was founded in 2010 with a simple mission: to provide the Treasure Valley with honest, reliable, and high-quality concrete services. What started as a small family operation has grown into one of the most trusted concrete contractors in the Boise metropolitan area, serving thousands of satisfied customers throughout Ada County and Canyon County.

Our founder, a third-generation concrete craftsman, moved to Meridian in 2009 and quickly recognized the need for a concrete contractor who truly cared about the community and stood behind their work. With over 20 years of experience in the concrete industry, he assembled a team of skilled professionals who shared his commitment to excellence, integrity, and customer satisfaction.

From our very first project - a residential driveway in the Meridian Highlands neighborhood - we've built our reputation one job at a time. Today, we're proud to have completed over 500 concrete installations and repairs throughout Meridian, Boise, Eagle, Star, Kuna, Nampa, and the surrounding Treasure Valley communities.

Our Values: The Foundation of Everything We Do

At Meridian Concrete Solutions, our values aren't just words on a website - they're the principles that guide every decision we make and every project we complete:

Quality Craftsmanship

We believe that concrete work is both an art and a science. Every pour, every finish, and every detail matters. Our team takes pride in delivering work that not only meets but exceeds industry standards. We use premium materials, follow best practices for Idaho's unique climate conditions, and never cut corners. When you choose us, you're choosing concrete work that will last for decades.

Integrity & Honesty

Transparency is at the core of our business. We provide detailed, written estimates with no hidden fees or surprise charges. If we encounter unexpected issues during a project, we communicate immediately and work with you to find the best solution. We've built our business on trust, and we honor that trust with every customer interaction.

Local Commitment

We understand the unique challenges of concrete work in Idaho's climate, from the freeze-thaw cycles to the soil conditions in the Treasure Valley. When you work with us, you're supporting a local family business that's invested in the success of our community.

Customer Focus

Every project is personal to us. We listen to your needs, answer your questions, and keep you informed throughout the entire process. From the initial estimate to the final walkthrough, we're committed to making your concrete project as smooth and stress-free as possible. Your satisfaction is our ultimate goal, and we stand behind our work with comprehensive warranties.

Meet Our Team

Our team is the heart of Meridian Concrete Solutions. We employ skilled concrete craftsmen, many of whom have been with us since the early days of our company. Our Commitment to Quality

Quality isn't accidental - it's the result of intentional choices at every stage of a project. Here's how we ensure the highest quality on every job:

Proper Site Preparation

We know that a concrete installation is only as good as the foundation beneath it. Our team thoroughly prepares every site, including proper excavation, grading, and compaction. We account for Idaho's soil conditions and drainage requirements to prevent future settling or cracking.

Premium Materials

We source our concrete from trusted local suppliers who provide consistent, high-quality mixes. For projects in our climate, we use air-entrained concrete that can withstand freeze-thaw cycles without deteriorating. We also use quality reinforcement materials, including rebar and wire mesh, to ensure structural integrity.

Expert Installation

Our craftsmen have years of experience and expertise in all types of concrete work. From setting forms to finishing surfaces, every step is executed with precision and care. We follow industry best practices and local building codes to ensure every installation is structurally sound and visually appealing.

Attention to Detail

The difference between good and great concrete work is in the details. We pay careful attention to joint placement, edge finishing, surface texture, and overall appearance. We take the time to do things right, because we know you'll be living with our work for many years to come.

Thorough Cleanup

We respect your property and treat it like our own. After completing the concrete work, we thoroughly clean the job site, removing all debris, forms, and excess materials. We leave your property in excellent condition, ready for you to enjoy your new concrete installation.
