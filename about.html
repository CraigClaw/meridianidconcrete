About Meridian Concrete Solutions

Meridian Concrete Solutions has been the Treasure Valley's trusted concrete contractor since 2010. Founded by a team of experienced concrete professionals who saw a gap in the Meridian market for honest, high-quality concrete work, we have grown from a two-person operation to a crew of 10+ serving the entire Boise metro area.

Our mission is simple: deliver concrete work that lasts, at fair prices, with honest communication from estimate to completion. No subcontractors, no surprise charges, no shortcuts.

Our Team

Every member of our team is a direct employee of Meridian Concrete Solutions. We do not use subcontractors. This means consistent quality on every project, from your initial estimate to the final walkthrough.

  • Fully licensed and insured — Idaho contractor license, $2M general liability, workers comp
  • Background-checked crews — Every team member is vetted and trained by us
  • Ongoing training — Our team stays current on concrete techniques, safety, and building codes
  • Local knowledge — We understand Treasure Valley soils, weather patterns, and permit requirements

Our Service Commitment

Transparent Pricing

Every estimate is detailed and itemized. You know exactly what you are paying for before we start. No hidden fees, no surprises.

Quality Materials

We use air-entrained concrete (6% minimum for all exterior work), proper reinforcement, and professional-grade sealers. We never cut corners on materials.

Respect for Your Property

We protect your landscaping, clean up daily, and leave your property looking better than we found it. Our crews are professional and courteous.

Written Warranty

Every project comes with a written warranty. We stand behind our work and are here if you ever need us.
