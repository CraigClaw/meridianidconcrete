Why Choose Concrete

Why Concrete Sidewalks Outperform Other Materials in Idaho

The Treasure Valley's climate is tough on outdoor surfaces. Summer temperatures routinely climb above 100°F, while winter brings repeated freeze-thaw cycles that can destroy asphalt and crack paver joints.

Freeze-Thaw Resistant

Concrete handles Idaho's repeated freeze-thaw cycles better than any alternative. It doesn't soften in heat and resists salt damage when properly sealed.

Long Lifespan

A well-installed concrete sidewalk in Meridian lasts 25 to 30 years with minimal maintenance, compared to asphalt which needs resealing every 2-3 years.

Low Maintenance

Your sidewalk needs only occasional cleaning and sealing every 5-7 years. For homeowners in Meridian, Eagle, and southwest Boise who want a surface they can walk on year-round, concrete is the practical choice.

Sidewalk Types

Residential & Commercial Sidewalk Solutions

Residential Walkways

Standard residential sidewalks are 4 feet wide and 4 inches thick, poured over a 4-inch compacted gravel base. We use 3,000 to 3,500 PSI concrete with fiber mesh reinforcement to resist cracking from freeze-thaw cycles and root intrusion from nearby landscaping. Control joints are cut every 4 to 5 feet to create planned weak points where the concrete can crack without visible damage.

Commercial ADA Walkways

Commercial properties face stricter requirements under the Americans with Disabilities Act. Key ADA requirements include minimum 36-inch clear width, maximum 1:20 slope, smooth slip-resistant surfaces, detectable warning surfaces at curb ramps, and level landings at doorways. We design and install ADA-compliant sidewalks for offices, retail stores, medical facilities, and restaurants throughout the Treasure Valley.

Decorative Options

Standard broom-finish concrete is practical and affordable, but decorative options elevate your property's appearance. Popular treatments include stamped concrete patterns like ashlar slate or cobblestone, exposed aggregate revealing natural stone, integral color in earth tones, and scored patterns creating the appearance of pavers. How We Work

The Installation Process

Every sidewalk we install follows the same rigorous process, whether it's a simple front walk in Kuna or a commercial ADA walkway in downtown Boise.

  1. 01
    Site assessment and measurement. We visit your property, take measurements, check soil conditions, and discuss your preferences for width, finish, and any decorative options.
  2. 02
    Base preparation. We excavate to proper depth, install and compact a 4-6 inch crushed gravel base, and set grading for water runoff — critical in Meridian where clay soils can cause heaving.
  3. 03
    Forming and reinforcement. Steel rebar or fiber mesh reinforcement, expansion joints at proper intervals, and edge forms ensure your sidewalk won't crack prematurely.
  4. 04
    Pouring and finishing. 3,500 PSI concrete delivered fresh from local plants. Your chosen finish is applied while the concrete is workable.
  5. 05
    Curing and sealing. We apply a curing compound and return after 28 days to apply a penetrating sealer that protects against Idaho's freeze-thaw cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Standard concrete sidewalks in Meridian typically cost $8-12 per square foot installed. A typical 4-foot wide, 50-foot long walkway (200 sq ft) runs $1,600-2,400. Decorative finishes like stamped patterns add $3-8 per square foot. We provide free detailed estimates for every project.

ADA requires minimum 36-inch clear width (48 inches preferred), maximum 1:20 slope (5%), smooth slip-resistant surfaces, detectable warning surfaces at curb ramps, and level landings at doorways. We design all commercial walkways to meet or exceed these federal requirements.

Most residential sidewalk projects are completed in 1-2 days. This includes excavation, base preparation, forming, pouring, and finishing. Concrete reaches initial set in 24-48 hours for light foot traffic, with full curing taking 28 days.

In Meridian and most Treasure Valley cities, new sidewalk installation typically requires a permit, especially for work in the public right-of-way. We handle all permit applications and coordinate with city inspectors for required inspections.
