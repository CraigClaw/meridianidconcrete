Nampa's Trusted Concrete Contractor

Meridian Concrete Solutions has been providing expert concrete services to Nampa homeowners, businesses, and agricultural properties for over 15 years. As one of the Treasure Valley's oldest cities, Nampa has a unique mix of concrete needs - from historic homes requiring careful concrete repair to new subdivisions on the west side demanding foundation work and driveways.

Our team understands Nampa's diverse concrete requirements. We serve established residential neighborhoods, commercial districts, and the agricultural properties that make Canyon County famous. Whether you need a simple sidewalk repair or a large shop floor pour, we have the equipment and expertise to handle projects of any scale.

Serving All Nampa Neighborhoods

We proudly serve every corner of Nampa, including:

  • Lakeview - Residential concrete services including driveways, patios, and sidewalks for this established neighborhood
  • Queens Landing - New construction foundations and custom decorative concrete for modern homes
  • Central Nampa - Older home concrete repair, driveway replacement, and foundation work
  • West Nampa - Growing area with new construction demand for all concrete services
  • Rural Nampa - Agricultural concrete including shop floors, equipment pads, and barn foundations

Agricultural Concrete Specialists

Nampa's agricultural heritage means we have extensive experience with farm and ranch concrete projects. We install heavy-duty shop floors designed to withstand equipment traffic, equipment pads for irrigation systems, barn and stable foundations, and concrete infrastructure for agricultural operations. Our team understands the unique requirements of agricultural concrete, including proper thickness, reinforcement, and finish for different applications.

Older Concrete Repair & Replacement

Many Nampa properties have concrete that was installed decades ago and is now showing its age. We specialize in assessing older concrete and determining whether repair or replacement is the best solution. Our repair services include crack injection, resurfacing, mudjacking for sunken slabs, and decorative overlays that can breathe new life into worn concrete surfaces.
