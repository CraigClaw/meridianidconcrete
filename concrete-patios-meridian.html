Why Choose Concrete

Why Choose Concrete for Your Patio

Concrete patios offer the perfect balance of durability, versatility, and value. Unlike wood decks that require annual staining or pavers that can shift over time, a properly installed concrete patio provides decades of low-maintenance enjoyment. Our Meridian customers trust us for quality work that lasts.

Weather Resistant

Concrete withstands Idaho's hot summers and freezing winters without warping, rotting, or shifting. Proper sealing protects against moisture and UV damage.

Design Flexibility

From smooth modern finishes to stamped patterns that mimic natural stone, concrete can be customized to match any architectural style.

Cost Effective

Concrete patios typically cost less per square foot than pavers or natural stone while offering comparable longevity and aesthetics.

How We Work

Our Patio Building Process

Every project we complete in the Treasure Valley follows our proven process for quality results.

  1. 01
    Design consultation. We discuss your vision, take measurements, and help you choose the best finish and color for your space.
  2. 02
    Site preparation. We excavate, grade for proper drainage, and install a compacted gravel base to prevent settling.
  3. 03
    Forming and pouring. Forms are set to your exact dimensions. We pour 4-inch thick slabs with reinforcement as needed.
  4. 04
    Finishing touches. Your chosen finish is applied, whether smooth, broomed, or stamped. We add control joints to manage cracking.
  5. 05
    Curing and sealing. After proper curing, we apply a sealer that protects your patio and enhances its appearance.
