Custom Concrete Patio Design & Installation

Outdoor living is a way of life in the Treasure Valley. With over 200 sunny days a year, Boise and Meridian homeowners invest heavily in their outdoor spaces. At Meridian Concrete Solutions, we design and install concrete patios that extend your living area outdoors while withstanding Idaho's extreme temperature swings — from scorching 100+ degree summers to sub-zero winters.

Our patios are engineered with the same freeze-thaw protection as our driveways: air-entrained concrete, proper base preparation, and professional sealing. The result is a patio that looks beautiful for decades without cracking, spalling, or settling — problems that plague poorly installed patios throughout the Boise metro area.

Patio Design Options for Idaho Homes

🎨 Stamped Concrete Patios

Stamped concrete transforms a plain patio into a natural stone surface at a fraction of the cost. Popular patterns in Boise include ashlar slate (mimics natural flagstone), cobblestone (European courtyard feel), and random stone. Stamped patios cost $10-$18 per square foot installed, compared to $25-$40 for natural stone. Available in dozens of colors that complement Idaho's mountain and desert landscapes.

🏢 Broom-Finish Patios

The most affordable option at $6-$9 per square foot. Broom-finish concrete provides a light texture that's slip-resistant around pools and in wet conditions. Many Boise homeowners choose broom-finish for large patios and add a stamped or stained border for visual interest without the full cost of decorative concrete.

✨ Exposed Aggregate Patios

Exposed aggregate reveals beautiful river rock and pea gravel embedded in the concrete surface. The textured surface provides excellent traction — perfect for pool decks and spa areas common in Eagle and Meridian backyards. Cost: $8-$14 per square foot. The natural stone look fits beautifully with Idaho's outdoor aesthetic.

🪨 Colored & Stained Patios

Integral color mixed into the concrete or acid staining applied after curing gives your patio a custom look. Popular Treasure Valley colors include sandstone (blends with desert landscape), terracotta (warm southwestern feel), and slate gray (modern mountain aesthetic). Colored patios cost $8-$12/sq ft for integral color and $10-$16/sq ft for acid staining.

Outdoor Living Trends in the Boise Metro Area

Boise was ranked among the top 10 fastest-growing cities in the US, and homeowners are investing in outdoor living spaces that let them enjoy Idaho's incredible seasons. Here are the patio features most requested by our Meridian and Boise customers:

  • Outdoor kitchens and fire pits — Extended patio areas with built-in fire pits are the #1 request. We can pour separate fire pit pads (heat-rated concrete) as part of your patio project.
  • Multi-level patios — Stepped or tiered patios that define different zones for dining, lounging, and cooking. Popular for homes with sloped lots in the Eagle foothills.

  • Patio borders and accents — Contrasting borders in a different stamp pattern or color add visual definition without the cost of a fully stamped patio.

Patio Maintenance for Idaho's Climate

Concrete patios in the Treasure Valley face intense UV exposure, 100+ freeze-thaw cycles per year, and de-icing salt exposure. Proper maintenance is essential:

  1. Seal every 2-3 years. UV rays break down sealers faster in high-altitude Boise. Use a penetrating silane/siloxane sealer for best protection against freeze-thaw damage.
  2. Keep furniture elevated in winter. Metal furniture legs can rust and stain the surface. Use furniture pads or store furniture during winter months.
  3. Avoid de-icing salts. Sodium chloride destroys concrete sealer and causes surface scaling. Use sand for traction instead.
  4. Clean spills promptly. BBQ grease, wine, and fertilizer can stain unsealed or worn sealer. Clean with a mild concrete cleaner.
  5. Re-seal stamped and stained patios. Decorative finishes lose color vibrancy without regular sealing. Budget $200-$400 for resealing every 2-3 years.
