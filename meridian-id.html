Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a permit for concrete work in Meridian, Idaho?

Yes, most concrete projects in Meridian require a building permit from the City of Meridian Building Department. This includes driveways, patios, sidewalks, and foundations. We handle all permit applications and coordinate inspections as part of our service.

What neighborhoods in Meridian do you serve?

We proudly serve all Meridian neighborhoods including Saguaro Canyon, Tuscany Village, Paramount, Settlers Bridge, Lochsa Falls, and many more. No matter where you live in Meridian, we're just minutes away.

How does Meridian's soil affect concrete installation?

Meridian has expansive clay soil that can shift with moisture changes. We account for this by installing proper base materials, using reinforced concrete where needed, and spacing control joints appropriately to prevent cracking from soil movement.
