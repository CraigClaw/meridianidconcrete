Commercial Concrete Contractor in Boise, Idaho

Meridian Concrete Solutions provides comprehensive commercial concrete services throughout the Treasure Valley. From tenant improvements and office building foundations to retail parking lots and industrial flatwork, we deliver concrete work that meets commercial building codes, timelines, and budgets.

Our commercial team has experience with projects ranging from 500 to 50,000+ square feet. We understand the unique requirements of commercial construction: ADA compliance, city permits, traffic control plans, and coordination with other trades.

Our Commercial Concrete Services

🏢 Building Foundations

Commercial-grade foundations for new construction, including spread footings, mat foundations, and foundation walls. We coordinate with structural engineers and building inspectors throughout the Boise metro area.

🅿️ Parking Lots & Flatwork

Commercial parking lots with proper drainage, ADA-compliant access aisles, and striping layout. We handle sidewalk approaches, curb and gutter, and ADA ramps throughout Ada and Canyon counties.

🏭 Industrial Floors

Heavy-duty concrete floors for warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers. Up to 6-inch thick, steel-reinforced, with fiber mesh options for crack resistance in high-traffic environments.

🏪 Retail & Restaurant

Concrete work for retail spaces including entryways, patios, dumpster pads, and loading dock approaches. We work with tenants, landlords, and general contractors on tight project schedules.

Commercial Project Management
